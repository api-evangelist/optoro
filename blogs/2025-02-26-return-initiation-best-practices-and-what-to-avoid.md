Returns are a big part of shopping, especially as online sales keep growing. How retailers handle the first step—return initiation—can make or break the customer experience. In this article, we’ll explore UX best practices and strategies for creating a seamless customer return experience (CRX) and driving return portal adoption, including insights from our Design Team’s latest retailer experience audit.
