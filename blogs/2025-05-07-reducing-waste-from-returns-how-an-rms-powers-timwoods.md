In today's dynamic retail landscape, retailers, brands, and third-party logistics (3PL) providers face complex challenges, balancing economic upheaval with evolving consumer expectations. Amidst this complexity, sustainability has emerged as a strategic imperative, not just an ethical consideration. While many think about powering the circular economy through manufacturing practices, looking at returned inventory can offer a more effective, achievable path to sustainability.
