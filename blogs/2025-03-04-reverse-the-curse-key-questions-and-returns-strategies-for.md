Returns are an integral part of the retail industry, significantly impacting customer loyalty, operational efficiency, and profitability. In 2024, the total value of returned goods reached approximately $890 billion, representing ~17% of all goods sold. Effectively managing returns is, therefore, essential for retailers aiming to thrive in 2025 and beyond.
